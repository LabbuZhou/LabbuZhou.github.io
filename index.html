I am a PhD candidate at Tianjin University and a visiting PhD student at Sapienza University of Rome. My research lies at the intersection of complex systems, transportation, and energy. Current work focuses on traffic dynamics, accessibility, sustainable mobility, and AI-enhanced transportation research, with a broader interest in combining empirical evidence, systems thinking, and computational approaches to better understand mobility systems.

Research Directions

  • Transportation systems modeling and traffic flow dynamics
  • Accessibility, urban mobility, and transportation equity
  • Transportation-energy systems and electric mobility
  • AI-enhanced transportation systems research
  • Complex systems and computational social science
  • Data-driven and behaviorally grounded mobility research

Research Portfolio

Selected public research stories. Each card states the research question, modelling perspective, and contribution without disclosing unpublished results.

Traffic Dynamics and Driving Behaviour

How individual decisions and heterogeneous behaviour generate collective traffic patterns.

Published · IEEE T-CYB · 2026

Driving-regime-aware modelling of intradriver heterogeneity

Develops a deep-learning framework that represents driving regimes explicitly when modelling multiscale car-following behaviour, making within-driver variability a central modelling object rather than residual noise.

Published · TR Part B · 2025

Calibrating stochastic car-following models

Provides a structured perspective on how data adequacy, parameter sensitivity, and objective choice jointly shape the calibration of stochastic car-following models.

Under Revision · TR Part B

Twenty-five years of the Intelligent Driver Model

Reviews the foundations, extensions, applications, and open questions of the Intelligent Driver Model, linking its classical formulation to contemporary traffic-modelling needs.

Under Review · Transportation Science

Scoring-rule theory for stochastic calibration

Investigates principled scoring-rule-based objectives for calibrating stochastic car-following models and comparing their predictive implications.

Mobility, Accessibility, and Urban Systems

How mobility systems distribute opportunity and how data-driven models can support equitable urban decisions.

Under Review · TR Part A

The moving target of urban equity

Examines the spatiotemporal nature of accessibility and double disadvantage in Hefei, asking how uneven access changes across people, places, and time.

Under Review · TUST

Adaptive ensemble learning for metro passenger flow

Combines clustering, decomposition, and ensemble forecasting with reinforcement-learning-based weighting to improve short-term metro passenger-flow prediction.

Under Review · IEEE T-ITS

Micro–macro calibration of car-following models

Connects microscopic trajectories and macroscopic traffic-flow characteristics in a calibration framework for heterogeneous car-following parameters.

Transportation, Energy, and AI-Enhanced Research

Connecting vehicle-level behaviour, environmental impacts, and AI methods for more interpretable transportation research.

Published · TR Part D · 2023

Emissions and fuel consumption in a car-following platoon

Uses controlled car-following experiments to characterize how platoon dynamics relate to emissions and fuel-consumption features at the vehicle level.

Published · Physica A · 2022

Multi-anticipative cellular automata and three-phase traffic

Introduces multi-anticipative effects into a cellular-automaton traffic model to reproduce empirical features associated with Kerner’s three-phase traffic theory.

Published · Frontiers of Engineering Management · 2026

Experiment-driven and AI-enhanced driving research

Articulates an experiment-driven, AI-enhanced research agenda for studying driving behaviour, bringing empirical design and computational modelling into a shared workflow.

Published · Springer · 2025

Cellular-automaton approaches to traffic systems

Presents cellular automata as an accessible modelling language for traffic systems, from local interaction rules to emergent network-level behaviour.
